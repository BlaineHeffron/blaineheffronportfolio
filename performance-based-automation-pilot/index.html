When it's fair

Conditions for performance-based pricing

  • The workflow has enough volume to actually measure.
  • The business can put a dollar value on the improvement.
  • I can get the access I need to build safely.
  • Both sides agree on what happens if the metric doesn't move.
Guardrails

How the deal stays clean

  1. Baseline first, before anything gets built.
  2. Payment trigger and attribution rules written down.
  3. One workflow. Support and handoff included.
  4. Results review before anyone talks about expanding.
Where it fits

Workflows that work for this

Slow quote follow-up. Missed lead response. Repetitive document work. Admin handoffs. Reporting where the time saved or the revenue at risk can be measured. Anywhere fuzzy, this isn't the right structure.

Metrics we'd track

  • Baseline volume
  • Time saved
  • Response speed
  • Recovered or protected revenue
