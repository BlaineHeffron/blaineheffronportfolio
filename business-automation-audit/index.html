When mapping beats automation

  • Several AI or automation ideas are on the table, but no obvious place to start.
  • Your team already has the tools. The problem is the handoff between them.
  • You want a practical scope before you green-light a build.
  • You need the review path and logging defined before anything touches live work.

How I narrow the first build

  1. List the tools, handoffs, and workarounds people are actually using.
  2. Pick the first practical wedge: Inbox-to-Action, Owner Weekly Digest, Operations Exception Board, or Invoice/Payment Drafting.
  3. Define what gets drafted, who reviews it, and what gets logged.
  4. Hand off a scoped build plan with a dry run before anything live changes.

What you walk away with

A process map in plain English. A ranked backlog. The first build I'd recommend, with a measurement plan attached. Everything starts off; we test each action once with no real change before enabling it, one at a time.

Numbers I dig into first

  • Manual review volume
  • Time spent per handoff
  • Draft accuracy in dry runs
  • Revenue risk from delays
