Where small teams feel it first

  • Orders, customers, products, invoices, and follow-up live in different tools.
  • A small team is running important operating work out of memory.
  • The owner wants better visibility without another weekly spreadsheet ritual.
  • You'd rather work with someone local who'll sit down with the workflow in person.

Keeping AI in its lane

  1. Look at the tools in place and the workarounds people quietly rely on.
  2. Pick one system tied to a business outcome the owner cares about.
  3. Use AI for drafting, routing, summarizing, or flagging. Not unreviewed decisions.
  4. Build the operations cockpit around how the team already works.

The first build depends on the business

Sometimes the right first project is Inbox-to-Action. Sometimes it is Owner Weekly Digest, Operations Exception Board, or Invoice/Payment Drafting. The diagnostic picks the right starting point instead of forcing the same automation on every business.

Numbers I watch early

  • Manual admin hours
  • Stuck orders or stale tasks
  • Error and rework rate
  • Owner time spent asking for updates
